About CampusTrace 🫡

The story behind the most useful website on campus (we think 😅)

Our Story 📖

It all started when one of us lost our earbuds in the library and nobody knew how to find them. We searched everywhere, asked everyone, posted on WhatsApp groups... nothing worked 😩

Then we thought - "Bro, we're CSE students! Why not make a website for this?" And that's how CampusTrace was born! 🚀

We built this in our hostel room during a weekend (instead of studying for midsems lol 😂). It's simple, it's basic, but it works! And that's what matters, right? 💯

What We Do 🤔

CampusTrace is a simple lost and found platform for LPU students. Here's what you can do:

✅ Report lost items with details and location

✅ Report found items so owners can claim them

✅ Search through all reported items

✅ Connect directly with the reporter

✅ Help your fellow students (good karma ✨)

No login required, no complicated stuff. Just fill the form and you're good to go! We keep it real 🫡

Why This Matters 💡

Every semester, hundreds of students lose their belongings on campus - phones, ID cards, keys, books, earbuds, you name it! 😱

Most of the time, someone has already found your item but doesn't know how to return it. CampusTrace bridges that gap! 🌉

Think of it as a digital notice board but way cooler and Gen Z friendly 😎✌️

Tech Stack 🛠️

We kept it simple because we're still learning (and also because we don't know React yet 😅):

🔸 HTML5 - Structure (the skeleton 💀)

🔸 CSS3 - Styling (making it look cool 🎨)

🔸 Vanilla JavaScript - Functionality (the brain 🧠)

🔸 localStorage - Data Storage (poor man's database 😂)

🔸 Google Fonts - Typography (Poppins supremacy 🔥)

Future plans: Add a proper backend with Node.js and MongoDB (once we learn it in 3rd semester probably 🤷‍♂️)
